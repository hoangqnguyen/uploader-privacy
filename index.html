Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy covers "uploader", a private publishing tool operated by Hoàng Nguyễn for his own social media accounts: the Proud Pit Family and PhiDr Facebook Pages and their linked Instagram, Threads and YouTube accounts. The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool does

It uses the official Meta Graph API, Threads API and YouTube Data API to publish our own videos, images, captions and comments to our own accounts, and to read back our own posts and comments to confirm they were published.

What data we access

  • Access tokens for our own Pages and accounts, stored only on our own server and never shared.
  • Basic information about our own Pages and channels (IDs, names) and our own posts and comments.

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of other people. The tool does not read messages, follower lists or other users' profiles, and does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Retention

We keep a local log of what we published (post IDs, links, timestamps) for operational purposes. It contains no third-party personal data.

Data deletion

The tool stores no data about other people. If you believe it holds data about you, or you want any data deleted, send a message to the Proud Pit Family or PhiDr Facebook Page and we will delete it within 30 days. You can also remove the app's access at any time in your Facebook, Instagram, Threads or Google account settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Google / YouTube

Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
