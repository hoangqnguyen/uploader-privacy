Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers Social Scheduler (also called "uploader"), a private publishing and community-management tool operated by Neuvis ("we", "us"), the business behind PhiDr and Proud Pit Family. Neuvis is the data controller for the data described here. It is used only for our own Facebook Pages and our own Instagram, Threads, YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is used only by us and is not offered to other businesses or to the public.

What the tool does

  • Publishes our own videos, images, captions and comments to our own accounts using the official Meta Graph API, Threads API, YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Reads comments and replies that people leave on our own posts, and messages sent to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account, so we can answer them. The tool never sends private messages.
  • On Threads, searches public posts by keyword and lists public posts that mention our profile, so a person on our team can decide whether to reply. Every reply is written or approved by a person. Nothing is posted automatically. We limit public replies to at most 10 per day and one reply per post.
  • Flags likely spam comments on our own posts so we can hide them.
  • Reads performance statistics (insights and analytics) for our own Pages and accounts.

Our own account credentials

Access tokens for our own accounts are stored only on our own access-restricted server and are never shared.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to see, correct or delete any data about you at any time. See our data deletion instructions. In short: email phidrapp@gmail.com or message the PhiDr or Proud Pit Family Facebook Page with the platform and your username. We delete any record about you within 30 days and confirm by reply. If you ask, we also delete our public reply to your post.
