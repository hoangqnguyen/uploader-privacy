Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Social Scheduler is a private tool operated by Neuvis for the PhiDr and Proud Pit Family accounts. It keeps a small internal log of public posts and comments we replied to, including the author's username, so that we never reply twice. To have any data about you deleted:

Option 1: ask us

  1. Email phidrapp@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion", or send a message to the PhiDr or Proud Pit Family Facebook Page.
  2. Tell us the platform (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube or TikTok) and your username or profile link. Add the post link if you also want our reply removed.
  3. Within 30 days we delete every record about you from our logs (and our reply, if you asked), then reply with a confirmation code.

Option 2: remove the app (only if you ever connected your own account)

  • Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → Social Scheduler → Remove.
  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → Social Scheduler → Remove, then "Send request" to ask for data deletion.
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → Remove.
  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Remove access.

Most people who interact with our posts have never connected an account to this tool. Option 1 covers you.

See also our Privacy Policy.
